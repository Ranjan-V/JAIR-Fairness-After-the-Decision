The unifying object is the label-specific contestation operator, concretely
summarized by (\kappa_g^+,\kappa_g^-) in the one-sided binary model.  It
links transformations, preservation, endogenous access, and allocation.
Identification requires separating its observed appellant component from the
latent error mass among non-appellants.

The work does not claim novelty for appeals, human review, recourse, fairness,
or constrained allocation in general.  The proposed wedge is their exact
combination: self-selected entry into a post-decision correction operator and
its consequences for group fairness.
