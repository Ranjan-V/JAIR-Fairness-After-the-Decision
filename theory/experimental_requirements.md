# Experimental requirements

Every experiment must report both (D_0) and (D_1) metrics, group-specific
(\alpha^+,\alpha^-,\rho^+,\rho^-\), their effective products, resource use,
and uncertainty across seeds.  Synthetic experiments must compare simulated
rates against analytic formulas rather than only plot qualitative trends.

Real-data studies are explicitly semisynthetic:

\[
\text{real classification task}+\text{simulated contestation mechanism}.
\]

They cannot support claims about historical appeal behavior.  Adult and German
Credit adapters require users to place legally obtained files locally.  Dataset
documentation, protected-attribute choices, label definitions, preprocessing,
and exclusions must be recorded.

Minimum checks include all boundary cases in EXP-10, crossed cost CDFs,
heterogeneous review, unequal base rates, rare groups, heavy-tailed costs, and
misspecified propensity/audit assumptions.  No configuration may be suppressed
because it weakens the preferred intervention.
